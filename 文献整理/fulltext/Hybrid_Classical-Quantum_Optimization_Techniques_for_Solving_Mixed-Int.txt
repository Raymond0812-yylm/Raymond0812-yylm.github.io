ABSTRACT Quantumcomputing(QC)holdsgreatpromisetoopenupaneweraofcomputingandhasbeen
receiving significant attention recently. To overcome the performance limitations of near-term QC, utilizing
the current quantum computers to complement classical techniques for solving real-world problems is of
utmost importance. In this article, we develop QC-based solution strategies that exploit quantum annealing
andclassicaloptimizationtechniquesforsolvinglarge-scaleschedulingproblemsinmanufacturingsystems.
Theapplicationsoftheproposedalgorithmsareillustratedthroughtwocasestudiesinproductionscheduling.
First,wepresentahybridQC-basedsolutionapproachforthejob-shopschedulingproblem.Second,wepro-
poseahybridQC-basedparametricmethodforthemultipurposebatchschedulingproblemwithafractional
objective. The proposed hybrid algorithms can tackle optimization problems formulated as mixed-integer
linear and mixed-integer fractional programs, respectively, and provide feasibility guarantees. Performance
comparison between state-of-the-art exact and heuristic solvers and the proposed QC-based hybrid solution
techniques is presented for both job-shop and batch scheduling problems. Unlike conventional classical
solutiontechniques, theproposedhybridframeworksharnessquantum annealing tosupplement established
deterministic optimization algorithms and demonstrate performance efficiency over standard off-the-shelf
optimization solvers.

I. INTRODUCTION
With the advent of technological advancements, quantum
computing (QC) has attracted widespread attention owing
to the realization of speedups offered by quantum tech-
niques for several complex computational problems [1].
This has led to transformational breakthroughs on certain
tasks achieved with near-term quantum devices [2], [3].
QC offers higher computational power than classical com-
puters [4] and could speed up solving complex optimiza-
tion problems. Scheduling is a major optimization problem
in systems operation planning and may serve as a testbed
for rigorous benchmarking of such quantum solution tech-
niques. As a result, several quantum-assisted algorithms
have been developed for solving complex scheduling prob-
lems [5] to validate their practical viability. Variants of
scheduling problems have also been tackled by quantum
approaches using both gate-model [6] and annealing-based
[7] devices. However, such approaches may not prove com-
petitive until the inadequacies of QC, including the lim-
ited number of qubits and limited error correction capabil-
ities, are overcome by further technological advancements
[8]. To this end, several hybrid quantum-classical solu-
tion techniques that harness the complementary strengths
of quantum and classical computers have been proposed
to tackle scheduling problems [9], [10]. Both tailored [11]
and general [12] hybrid QC-based optimization techniques

II. QUANTUM ANNEALING PRELIMINARIES

computation proceeds by continuous-time evolution of a
quantum state from a known initial state to a final state un-
der adiabatic conditions [23]. The gradual transition from
an initial Hamiltonian to a final Hamiltonian to obtain the
ground state of the final Hamiltonian as governed by the
adiabatic evolution path is the main idea behind AQC [24].
Inpractice,however,nonidealitiesexhibitedbythequantum
physical systems lead to difficulties in realizing the strin-
gent adiabatic conditions required for operations in AQC.

the energy landscape and escape local minima [25]. Certain

Hamiltonian is introduced while the initial Hamiltonian’s

III. JOB-SHOP SCHEDULING
In this section, we demonstrate the effectiveness of QC-
based solution methodologies by developing a hybrid de-
compositionalgorithmforthejob-shopschedulingproblem.
The job-shop scheduling problem aims to find an optimal
scheduling assignment for the jobs on the machines given
a set of operational constraints. While exact optimization
algorithms based on the branch-and-cut method exist for
job-shop scheduling, they could exhibit inefficiencies for
industrial-scaleproblems[31],[32].Moreover,thequalityof
thesolutionsprovidedbyapproximatealgorithms,whichuse
heuristics like simulated annealing, is not guaranteed [33].
Hybriddecompositionmethodsthatuseconstraintprogram-
ming(CP)andMILPtolowerthecomplexityofthisproblem
have been proposed [34]. Furthermore, a hybrid decompo-
sition strategy that integrates QC with classical computing
hasbeenshowntosolvelargejob-shopschedulinginstances
[10].However,solvingindustrial-scaleinstancesusingthese
algorithmsmaytakeaprohibitivelylongtime.Weproposean
improvedapproachthatleveragesmoreefficientdecomposi-
tions to leverage QC-based techniques and solve job-shop
scheduling problems at industrial scales within a reasonable
time and with minimal space requirements.

IV . BATCH PROCESS SCHEDULING
Multipurpose batch scheduling in batch manufacturing is
representative of the scheduling problems emerging in in-
dustries.Thisprobleminvolvesassigningtaskstoequipment
resources, sequencing tasks on each equipment unit, and
determining the optimal batch sizes. The batch scheduling
problemcomprisesvariousresourceandstorageconstraints.
MIFP scheduling problems are widely applicable in process
systems engineering [44]. Exact solution methods based on
linear programming reformulation for linear fractional pro-
grams have been proposed [45]. Because MIFP problems
are a special class of mixed-integer nonlinear programming
(MINLP)problems,general-purposeMINLPalgorithmscan
beusedtosolveMIFPproblems[46].Duetothecomplexity
of its search space, this MIFP scheduling problem belongs
to the class of NP-hard optimization problems, and it is in-
tractable to optimize on a large-scale using general-purpose
MINLPmethods.Toovercomesuchdifficulties,wepropose
a novel approach that integrates an inexact parametric algo-
rithm [47] with a QC-based strategy to solve this optimiza-
tion problem.

3) SOLUTION APPROACH
We introduce a solution approach for the batch scheduling
problemthatemploystwoiterativeprocesses.Theprimaryor
theouteriterationdealswithsolvingtheschedulingproblem
witha linear objective in(30) tooptimality and updating the
parameter value. The secondary or the inner process solves
the relaxed parametric problem with a MILP solver, fol-
lowed by searching for feasible equipment allocations with
a quantum annealer in an iterative manner. The proposed
solution approach is summarized in Fig. 4. We integrate an
efficient parametric algorithm with the hybrid decomposi-
tion approach to tackle the multipurpose batch scheduling
problem. The initial value of dynamic parameterλ is set to
zero, and a tolerance valueδ is specified. The fractional ob-
jective for the original MIFP problem is computed using the
solutionobtainedfromtheinneriterativeprocess.Everypri-
mary iteration in the algorithm also involves computing the
absolute difference betweenλ and the objective. The algo-
rithmconvergesandreturnsanoptimalsolutionfortheMIFP
problem if the tolerance level is larger than this difference.
Otherwise,thealgorithmmovesontothenextouteriteration
by updating λ to the value of the last computed fractional
objective.
Within each inner iteration, the start times, finish times,
andbatchsizesoftasksareobtainedaftersolvingtherelaxed
parametricproblem.Nosolutionexistsforthebatchschedul-
ing problem if the relaxed problem is proven infeasible and
the algorithm stops. The optimal schedule for equipment
assignments is determined by solving the QUBO problems.
The main idea behind this is that if the equipment resources
cannot be balanced, then the partial solutions returned from
the relaxed parametric problem are not valid. For any equip-

return infeasible allocation sequences. The quality of the
integer cuts added to the relaxed MILP after performing the
feasibility checks is important for the hybrid algorithm to
converge within a reasonable time. Subsequently, when no
feasible schedules exist for equipmentr and the assigned

V . CONCLUSION
In this article, we proposed two hybrid QC-based optimiza-
tion techniques for solving large-scale MILP and MIFP
scheduling problems. The proposed algorithms exploited
thecomplementarystrengthsofclassicaloptimizationmeth-
ods and quantum annealing. The applicability and perfor-
mance of the hybrid methods were demonstrated through
twowell-knownschedulingproblemsinindustrialmanufac-
turing, namely job-shop scheduling and multipurpose batch
scheduling. For the job-shop scheduling problem, we devel-
oped a novel hybrid QC-MILP approach. In the proposed
solutiontechnique,relaxedMILPproblemsweresolvedwith
anoff-the-shelfMILPsolvertofindpossibleoptimalassign-
mentsofjobsonmachines,andQUBOproblemsweresolved
with a quantum annealer to search for feasible schedules
basedonthoseassignments.Wedevelopedanovelalgorithm
that integrates a hybrid QC-based scheme with a paramet-
ric algorithm to solve the multipurpose batch scheduling
