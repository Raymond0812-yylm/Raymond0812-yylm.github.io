面向可扩展超导量子处理器的钽空气桥

Tantalum airbridges for scalable superconducting quantum processors

量子硬件平台 🏭 制造调度🧪 材料化工

核心内容

钽膜因其优异的相干时间与耐酸碱性成为超导量子比特的关键材料。论文提出新型剥离法制造钽空气桥(控制线跨接、接地与频带抑制结构),并完成系统表征。工艺级创新是超导平台扩大比特规模的隐形基础,直接关系量子优化硬件的保真度上限。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1077 字符)

The unique property of tantalum, particularly its exceptional resistance to both acid and alkali, makes it promising for superconducting quantum processors. Here, we propose a novel lift-off method for fabricating tantalum airbridges with separate or fully-capped structures. This method introduces an aluminum film as a barrier layer to separate two layers of photoresist, which is then etched away before depositing tantalum film. We experimentally characterize these tantalum airbridges as control line jumpers, ground plane crossovers and coupling elements, and further validate the overall adaptability by a 13-qubit quantum processor with a median T 1 exceeding 100 μs. The median single-qubit gate fidelity is measured at 99.95(2)% for isolated Randomized Benchmarking and 99.94(2)% for the simultaneous one. Additionally, the experimental achievement of airbridge coupling with a controlled-Z gate fidelity surpassing 99.2(2)% in a separate two-qubit quantum chip may facilitate scalable quantum computation and quantum error correction with entirely tantalum elements.
