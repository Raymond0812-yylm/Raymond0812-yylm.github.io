带额外副本间相互作用的阻挫增强量子退火纠错模型

Frustration-enhanced quantum annealing correction models with additional inter-replica interactions

量子退火与绝热优化

核心内容

量子退火纠错(QAC)通过多副本编码保障硬件上Ising基态的鲁棒性。田中宗团队研究在QAC中引入额外副本间相互作用(如阻挫型耦合)的效果,分析其对高阻挫问题的纠错能力与能隙结构的影响,为退火硬件的误差缓解提供了新的编码设计。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 903 字符)

Quantum annealing correction (QAC) models provide a promising approach for mitigating errors in quantum annealers. Previous studies have established that QAC models are crucial for ensuring the robustness of the ground state of the Ising model on hardware. In this work, the effects of QAC models incorporating replicas with additional interactions, specifically, the and the , are investigated for problems characterized by a small energy gap between the ground and first excited states during quantum annealing, a well-known bottleneck to reaching the ground state. The results demonstrate that these QAC models can obtain the optimal solution within short annealing times by exploiting diabatic transitions, even for problems with a small energy gap. These findings highlight the potential of QAC models as practical near-term algorithms for hardware subject to runtime limitations and control noise.
