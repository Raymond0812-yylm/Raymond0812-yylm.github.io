面向二元喷漆车间问题的免优化递归QAOA

Optimization-free recursive Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm for the binary paint shop problem

核心内容

递归QAOA(RQAOA)可缓解QAOA在对称局域哈密顿量上的性能瓶颈并缩减电路,但仍需变分参数优化。论文针对二元喷漆车间问题提出免优化的RQAOA:利用参数迁移彻底去除变分循环,在保持解质量的同时大幅降低运行成本,展示了RQAOA在工业调度问题上的实用潜力。

The Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) is a leading candidate for near-term quantum advantage, yet its practical impact is hindered by limited performance on symmetric local Hamiltonians and the costly optimization of variational parameters. The recursive-QAOA (RQAOA) introduced by Bravyi [] addresses the first limitation while also reducing circuit size, and parameter transfer techniques can be used to bypass the optimization loop effectively. In this work, we combine these two ideas to develop an optimization-free RQAOA and evaluate its performance on the binary paint shop problem (BPSP)—an optimization problem found in manufacturing where a sequence of cars must be painted under constraints while minimizing the number of color changes. The BPSP can be formulated as an Ising ground state problem with a symmetric local Hamiltonian in the form of MAX-CUT and properties well suited for the application of QAOA parameter transfer. We benchmark QAOA and RQAOA with parameter transfer against classical solvers and heuristics, and investigate their resilience to suboptimal parameters. For circuit optimization, we use reverse-causal cones and introduce a method of trimming outer two-qubit gates.
