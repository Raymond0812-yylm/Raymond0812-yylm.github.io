具有全连接可重构路由器的模块化量子处理器

Modular Quantum Processor with an All-to-All Reconfigurable Router

量子近似优化 量子硬件平台

核心内容

Physical Review X 工作:提出可重构全连接路由器的模块化超导量子处理器架构,突破平面限制的比特连接性,对 QAOA 等需要非平凡连接拓扑的优化算法尤为重要。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1500 字符)

Superconducting qubits provide a promising approach to large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computing. However, qubit connectivity on a planar surface is typically restricted to only a few neighboring qubits. Achieving longer-range and more flexible connectivity, which is particularly appealing in light of recent developments in error-correcting codes, however, usually involves complex multilayer packaging and external cabling, which is resource intensive and can impose fidelity limitations. Here, we propose and realize a high-speed on-chip quantum processor that supports reconfigurable all-to-all coupling with a large on-off ratio. We implement the design in a four-node quantum processor, built with a modular design comprising a wiring substrate coupled to two separate qubit-bearing substrates, each including two single-qubit nodes. We use this device to demonstrate reconfigurable controlled- Z gates across all qubit pairs, with a benchmarked average fidelity of 96.00 % ± 0.08 % and best fidelity of 97.14 % ± 0.07 % , limited mainly by dephasing in the qubits. We also generate multiqubit entanglement, distributed across the separate modules, demonstrating GHZ-3 and GHZ-4 states with fidelities of 88.15 % ± 0.24 % and 75.18 % ± 0.11 % , respectively. This approach promises efficient scaling to larger-scale quantum circuits and offers a pathway for implementing quantum algorithms and error-correction schemes that benefit from enhanced qubit connectivity.
