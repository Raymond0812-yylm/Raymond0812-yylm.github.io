量子退火特征选择与PCA结合的混合降维用于碳排放预测

Improving dimensionality-reduction for carbon-emissions prediction using hybrid quantum annealing-based feature selection and PCA

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1500 字符)

Accurate carbon-emissions prediction is critical for evidence-based climate policy, where even modest improvements can influence emission–reduction targets. However, high-dimensional datasets often exhibit noise, nonlinear dependencies, and multicollinearity that degrade model stability. We propose QMICO-PCA, a hybrid dimensionality-reduction method that integrates quantum annealing (QA)-based feature selection with principal component analysis (PCA). In the selection stage, mutual information (MI) and Pearson correlation (CO) are combined multiplicatively within a QUBO formulation to jointly reward nonlinear relevance and penalize linear redundancy, enabling balanced subset optimization. The selected features are then transformed via PCA to enhance generalization and mitigate overfitting. We evaluate QMICO-PCA against classical approaches (RFE, LASSO, ELN), with and without PCA, using three regression models on the Our World in Data carbon dataset (13,706 records; 79 features; 1970–2023). Experiments are conducted on heterogeneous QA-based solvers to assess optimization robustness. QMICO-PCA achieves the strongest overall performance (average RMSE = 1134.12, MAE = 237.54, $$R^2$$ = 0.8020), corresponding to a 4-7% error reduction over classical methods. In multi-year emission prediction, such reductions can lead to lower cumulative projection errors, supporting more reliable quantitative policy design.
