企业级优化基准上的经典、量子启发与启发式求解器对比

Benchmarking Classical, Quantum-Inspired, and Heuristic Solvers on Enterprise Optimization Benchmarks

量子启发式算法 🏭 制造调度

核心内容

以作业车间调度等企业相关优化为基准,对经典、量子启发与启发式求解器进行可复现的严格对比,为求解器选型提供依据,发表于 JAIF。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 975 字符)

Optimization is central to enterprise decision support, yet solver selection is increasingly complex in the era of classical, quantum-inspired, and heuristic approaches.This paper conducts a rigorous, reproducible benchmarking study across enterprise-relevant optimization benchmarks-job shop scheduling, vehicle routing, and resource allocation-to compare classical exact solvers, quantum-inspired heuristics, and traditional heuristics.We evaluate solution quality, runtime, memory footprint, and scalability across problem size tiers, constraint structures, and instance varieties.Our results reveal trade-offs and performance patterns that inform enterprise decision-making in DSS, ERP, and operations planning contexts.The study emphasizes reproducibility through open benchmarks, transparent configurations, and detailed reporting, contributing both theoretically (taxonomy, benchmarking protocol) and practically (decision guidance, enterprise-ready evaluation model).
