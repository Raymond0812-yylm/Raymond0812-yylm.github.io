固定汉明重量子空间的量子编码器

Quantum encoder for fixed-Hamming-weight subspaces

量子硬件平台

核心内容

提出精确的 n 量子比特计算基幅度编码器,将实/复数据向量编码进固定汉明重量子空间,实现 k 次多项式空间压缩且线路最优,发表于 Physical Review Applied,对组合优化的量子数据加载具有直接价值。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1500 字符)

We present an exact $n$-qubit computational-basis amplitude encoder of real- or complex-valued data vectors of $d=(\genfrac{}{}{0}{}{n}{k})$ components into a subspace of fixed Hamming weight $k$. This represents a polynomial space compression of degree $k$. The circuit is optimal in that it expresses an arbitrary data vector using only $d\ensuremath{-}1$ (controlled) reconfigurable beam splitter (RBS) gates. It is constructed by an efficient classical algorithm that sequentially generates all bitstrings of weight $k$ and identifies the gates that superpose the corresponding states with the correct amplitudes. An explicit compilation into $\mathrm{CNOT}\mathrm{s}$ and single-qubit gates is presented, with the total $\mathrm{CNOT}$-gate count of $\mathcal{O}(kd)$ provided in analytical form. In addition, we show how to load data in the binary basis by sequentially stacking encoders of different Hamming weights using $\mathcal{O}(d\mathrm{log}﻿(d))\phantom{\rule{0.2em}{0ex}}\mathrm{CNOT}$ gates. Moreover, using generalized RBS gates that mix states of different Hamming weights, we extend the construction to efficiently encode arbitrary sparse vectors. We perform a proof-of-principle demonstration of our scheme on a commercial trapped-ion quantum computer. We successfully upload a $q$-Gaussian probability distribution in the non-log-concave regime with $n=6$ and $k=2$.
