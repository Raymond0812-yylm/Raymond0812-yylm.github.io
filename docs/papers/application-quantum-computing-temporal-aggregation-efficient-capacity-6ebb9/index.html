量子计算辅助的时间聚合方法用于电源容量扩展规划

Application of quantum computing to temporal aggregation for efficient capacity expansion in power systems

量子近似优化 量子-经典混合优化 ⚡ 能源电力

核心内容

容量扩展规划(CEP)需全年逐小时时序刻画负荷与新能源波动,计算代价高昂;该文把"代表日选择"重构为 QUBO 用量子-经典框架求解,在保留时序代表性的同时大幅压缩问题规模。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1500 字符)

Capacity expansion planning (CEP) requires full-year, hourly time series to capture variability in load and renewable generation, but this temporal resolution makes optimization problems computationally challenging. a quantum–classical temporal aggregation framework has been proposed that formulates representative-day selection as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problem and solves it using the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA). To keep each QUBO within practical simulator or near-term hardware limits (about 15 qubits), the year is partitioned into 24 half-month windows; each window is reduced to k representative days via QAOA-based clustering and assigned non-uniform weights proportional to the sizes of their clusters. The weighted days are then integrated into a CEP model built on PyPSA (Python for Power System Analysis) with linearized optimal power flow. Evaluations on IEEE 9, 30, and 118 bus systems show that small sets of QAOA-selected, non-uniformly weighted representative days preserve planning-cost fidelity within 4%–6% of the full-resolution benchmark while reducing the temporal dimension by over 75%. This yields substantially faster CEP solves after a one-time clustering step. Our contribution is a structured, optimization-based approach to temporal aggregation that embeds a QUBO–QAOA subroutine within a conventional CEP workflow.
