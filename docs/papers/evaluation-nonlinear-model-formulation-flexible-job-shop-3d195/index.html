制造柔性作业车间调度的非线性模型形式化评估

Evaluation of Nonlinear Model Formulation for Flexible Job Shop Scheduling in Manufacturing Using Quantum Annealing

核心内容

评估 FJSSP 的非线性模型(NLM)形式化:混合非线性求解器与既有二元二次模型(BQM)对比 makespan 与计算时间,发表于 Procedia CIRP。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1098 字符)

This study evaluates a nonlinear model (NLM) formulation for flexible job shop scheduling problems (FJSSP) using a hybrid nonlinear solver and compares achieved makespan and computing times against established binary quadratic model (BQM) approaches. The NLM formulation enables non-iterative computation across all tested problem instances, eliminating the need for decomposition required by BQM-based methods. However, solution quality remains inferior to both iterative hybrid BQM and quantum processing unit (QPU) approaches. Computational analysis reveals that the NLM approach exhibits higher computation times for smaller instances compared to iterative hybrid BQM methods but demonstrates more favorable scaling behavior between smallest and largest instances. The results suggest potential application of NLM formulations for generating initial solutions in large-scale scheduling problems. Additional findings indicate that minimal BQM energy does not necessarily correspond to minimal makespan solutions, and chain breaks in QPU implementations can occasionally yield feasible solutions.
