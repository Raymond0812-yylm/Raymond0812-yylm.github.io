面向量子电池应用的比特平台能量学分类

Energy‐Based Classification of Qubit Platforms for Quantum Battery Applications

量子硬件平台 🧪 材料化工

核心内容

以能量学视角把量子电池候选平台分为无机、分子与有机三大材料族,按能级可调性、退相干机制与环境敏感性系统比较;超导与硅自旋比特在控制精度上突出,发表于 Advanced Quantum Technologies。

原始摘要

展开 / 收起英文摘要(共 1311 字符)

ABSTRACT This review classifies qubit platforms from the perspective of quantum battery applications as inorganic, molecular, and organic material families using an energy‐based approach. The systems are compared in terms of energy level tunability, decoherence mechanisms, and environmental sensitivity. Superconducting and silicon spin qubits stand out with their high control accuracy and long decoherence times (on the order of seconds in purified silicon); however, the need for cryogenic operation and fixed energy levels limits them. In contrast, molecular (lanthanide and transition‐metal complexes) and organic (radical/polymer) systems offer atomically precise tunable energy levels through ligand engineering and chemical design and operate under near‐room temperature conditions. The study evaluates qubit platforms not only in terms of information processing but also with battery‐focused metrics such as energy capacity, charge power, ergotropy, storage lifetime, and quantum advantage arising from entanglement and collective interactions. Thus, molecular and organic systems stand out as the most promising candidates for quantum batteries due to their chemical tunability and bulk charging potential; the study presents a materials‐based roadmap combining quantum computing with energy storage.
